flexible blocker

Change restrictions

Choose what gets blocked, and when it is allowed through.

Any domain containing a restricted expression may get blocked. For example, if you block facebook, then facebook.fakedomain, dfacebook.com and www.facebook.com are all blocked too — so don't block something like a. You can allow sites to be visited at certain times of day, days of the week, or days of the month.

It is recommended that you set up minimal restrictions, blocking things only at certain times or using warn only instead of hard blocks. This prevents you from feeling the urge to disable the blocker or modify its settings later on.
